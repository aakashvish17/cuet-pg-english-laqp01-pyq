1. T.S. Eliot — "Dissociation of Sensibility" Essay & Century ▼

Appeared in: 2017, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2026 (Recurred 5 times)

Correct Answer: Coined in "The Metaphysical Poets" (1921). Set in during the 17th century after John Donne and Andrew Marvell. Eliot blamed John Milton and John Dryden for aggravating this separation of thought from feeling.
2. John Keats — "Negative Capability" Origin & Exemplar ▼

Appeared in: 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024, 2026 (Exact 2-Year Pulse!)

Correct Answer: Formulated in an 1817 letter to his brothers George and Thomas Keats. Keats defined it as being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts without irritable reaching after fact and reason. Supreme model: William Shakespeare.
3. Aristotle's Poetics — Four Formative Tragic Terms ▼

Appeared in: 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022, 2025, 2026

Correct Matches:
• Hamartia: Tragic flaw or error of judgment.
• Catharsis: Purgation / purification of pity and fear.
• Anagnorisis: Recognition from ignorance to knowledge.
• Peripeteia: Sudden reversal of situation / fortune.
4. Shakespeare — Chronological Order of Four Great Tragedies ▼

Appeared in: 2018, 2020, 2023, 2024, 2026

Mnemonic Formula: H - O - L - M
1. Hamlet (c. 1601)
2. Othello (c. 1603)
3. King Lear (c. 1605)
4. Macbeth (c. 1606)
5. Mulk Raj Anand — "Untouchable" (1935) Key Dimensions ▼

Appeared in: 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024, 2026 (2-Year Alternate Cycle)

Core Facts: Protagonist is Bakha (18-year-old sweeper boy in Bulashah). Action covers a single day. Historical preface contributed by British novelist E.M. Forster.
6. Raja Rao — "Kanthapura" (1938) Narrative Structure ▼

Appeared in: 2017, 2019, 2022, 2025, 2026

Core Facts: Narrated by elderly village grandmother Achakka. Narrative style uses traditional Indian Harikatha / Sthala-Purana oral storytelling. Local Gandhian leader is Moorthy.
🎯 30-Day Step-by-Step Study Roadmap

Designed specifically for students starting from limited preparation to reach 220+ marks:

1
Phase 1 (Days 1–7): Literary Terms & Critical Concepts

• Study M.H. Abrams' core Literary Terms (Dramatic Monologue, Soliloquy, Oxymoron, Paradox, Metonymy, Synecdoche, Bildungsroman, Picaresque).
• Memorize the Theorist → Term Table (Eliot, Keats, Coleridge, Aristotle, Wimsatt, Barthes, Derrida, Said, Bhabha, Showalter).
• Score Impact: Secures 25–30 marks immediately from direct definition and matching questions.

2
Phase 2 (Days 8–15): Indian Writing in English

• Novelists: Mulk Raj Anand (Untouchable, Coolie), R.K. Narayan (Malgudi, The Guide), Raja Rao (Kanthapura).
• Poets: Nissim Ezekiel (Night of the Scorpion), Kamala Das (An Introduction), A.K. Ramanujan, Toru Dutt.
• Dramatists: Girish Karnad (Tughlaq, Hayavadana), Mahesh Dattani.
• Score Impact: 16%–18% of the entire paper comes from this highly predictable, factual domain.

3
Phase 3 (Days 16–22): Major British Chronology & Canon

• Memorize English Literary Ages in chronological order.
• Shakespeare: HOLM tragedy sequence, Sonnet sequence (154 total, 1–126 Fair Youth, 127–152 Dark Lady), First Folio (1623).
• Milton: Paradise Lost (1667/1674), Areopagitica (1644).
• Romantics: Lyrical Ballads (1798), Wordsworth's Preface (1800), Keats's Odes (1819).

4
Phase 4 (Days 23–30): Timed PYQ Practice & Error Log

• Solve official CUET-PG papers (2022 to 2026) under a strict 90-minute timer.
• Maintain a physical notebook Error Log: note down every question answered incorrectly and revise its underlying concept the same day.

The Match-the-Following Scoring Hack:
Because over 35% of the exam uses List-I / List-II matching with 4 options, you almost never need to know all 4 items! Knowing just 2 items eliminates 3 out of 4 multiple-choice options in 90% of cases, securing +4 marks without risk.

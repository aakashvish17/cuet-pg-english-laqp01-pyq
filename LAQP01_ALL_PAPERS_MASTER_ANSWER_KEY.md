## 📌 Executive Summary & Answer Key Policy

1. **Official NTA Final Answer Keys**: The National Testing Agency (NTA) publishes official "Final Answer Keys" after reviewing all candidate challenges. These finalized keys form the sole basis of score calculation.
2. **Dropped Questions Policy ("DROP")**: When an exam question contains factual ambiguity, multiple conflicting keys, or incorrect question phrasing, NTA marks the question as **"DROP"**. Under official NTA rules:
   * **Full Marks (+4)** are awarded to **ALL candidates** who appeared in that shift, regardless of whether they attempted it or what option they selected.
   * Example: **Q6 (QBID 1011006) in CUET-PG 2022** and **Q64 (QID 92090624064) in CUET-PG 2023** were officially marked as `DROP`.
3. **Decoded Option Numbers**: Official NTA files present answers as raw Option IDs (e.g. `8048777202`). In this master repository, all Option IDs have been mathematically and empirically decoded into straightforward **Option Numbers (1, 2, 3, or 4 / A, B, C, D)** alongside the verified literary fact.
